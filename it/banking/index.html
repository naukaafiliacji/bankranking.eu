Account costs and welcome rewards

Compare monthly account and card charges first. “0 / X” shows the fee with and without the stated waiver. Expanded details show annual maintenance, eligibility, other charges and official sources. Introductory fee discounts are explained separately; transactions, taxes and optional services can cost extra.

Welcome rewards belong to the customer and may require salary credit, spending, an application code or a commitment period. Cash, vouchers and physical gifts are labelled separately. They are not deducted from annual fees. Availability may end before the advertised deadline if limits or stock are reached. A zero welcome figure means no cash welcome reward is listed for this specific product in the offer reviewed. Gifts, vouchers, deposit interest and publisher commissions are excluded. Every personal-list bank also appears in the other account-type tabs; catalogue entries are identified separately and are not priced as personal accounts.
