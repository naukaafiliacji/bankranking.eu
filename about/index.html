About BankRanking

How BankRanking researches, updates and separates editorial comparisons from commercial relationships.

  • We compare banking products in Poland, Spain and Italy using a documented methodology.
  • Product information is checked against primary sources where possible, including provider documents and regulator publications.
  • Commercial relationships do not determine editorial ranking positions.
  • Pages show update dates and readers can report corrections at contact@bankranking.eu.

Editorial process

We define the comparison question, collect product terms, normalize the criteria and review the result before publication. Material changes trigger a new review.

Sources and corrections

Primary provider documents are preferred for product terms. Regulatory and central-bank sources are used for market context. Readers can flag a correction and we review the underlying source.

Affiliate transparency

BankRanking may use affiliate links. A commercial relationship can affect whether a provider is monetized, but it does not change the editorial criteria or the factual description of the product.
