Research governance

Research

How BankRanking Europe maps banking markets, prioritises evidence, normalises product data and keeps commercial incentives separate from the research process.

01 · Research charter

Research should be reproducible enough to challenge

BankRanking Europe is built around a simple principle: a ranking is more credible when the reader can understand what was compared, where the facts came from, which assumptions were applied and what could cause the result to change. Our research process is therefore designed to leave an evidence trail rather than rely on opaque editorial intuition.

The goal is not to claim perfect objectivity. The goal is procedural objectivity: equivalent products should face equivalent evidence rules, data definitions and ranking logic.
02 · Market universe

Coverage starts with the banking market, not the affiliate catalogue

For each country we first identify providers that materially matter to the intended customer segment. The research universe can draw on national supervisory registers, central banks, banking associations, annual reports, customer/reach evidence and established local market sources.

A provider may be important in one category and irrelevant in another. Inclusion is therefore category-specific: a major business bank does not automatically belong in a children's-account ranking, and a specialist broker does not automatically belong in the bank-first investment view.

04 · Research workflow

A controlled path from source to ranking

StepControl
1. Define scopeCountry, product category and target customer segment are fixed before the ranking is reviewed.
2. Build provider universeMajor market participants are mapped independently of affiliate availability.
3. Collect evidenceRanking-critical facts are captured from the highest-quality available sources.
4. Normalise dataFees, rates, limits, eligibility and features are converted into consistent field definitions.
5. Resolve conflictsWhere sources disagree, the more authoritative and more recent evidence is preferred and ambiguity is not silently converted into certainty.
6. Apply category logicPersonal, business, student, children's, savings and investment products are assessed using different factor sets.
7. Review outliersUnexpected results are checked for data errors; the methodology is not changed merely to move a preferred provider.
8. Publish with contextThe page shows rank order, comparison factors, Best for, key details and source links instead of a false-precision decimal score.
9. Monitor material changesRates, promotions, fees and product withdrawals can trigger a recheck.
10. Correct transparentlyMaterial factual errors are corrected and, where relevant, the ranking is recalculated.
05 · Category models

Different products require different questions

Personal accounts

Recurring costs, card/ATM conditions, digital access, day-to-day usability, eligibility and relevant welcome offers.

Business accounts

Recurring fees, transfers, cards/team access, bookkeeping integrations and business-specific account tools.

Student / youth / children

Age eligibility, fee waivers, parental controls, learning features, travel use and transition to adult products.

Savings

Rate, duration, balance limits, liquidity, new-money conditions, deposit structure and accessibility.

Fixed-term deposits

Term, rate, minimum/maximum balance, early-access constraints and maturity handling.

Investing

Product access, custody, trading/fund costs, service model and investor profile, with banks separated from specialist platforms.

06 · Freshness & evidence dates

Time is part of the data

A savings rate or promotion without a verification date is incomplete evidence. BankRanking treats rankings as dated research snapshots. High-volatility fields such as promotional offers and savings rates deserve more frequent rechecking than slow-changing descriptive fields such as branch-network positioning.

07 · Promotions

Headline value is not the same as expected value

“Up to” rewards are treated as maximum potential values unless the conditions make a lower realistic value appropriate. We distinguish between guaranteed benefits and contingent benefits, record campaign deadlines where available, and consider whether activity requirements could materially reduce the practical value of an offer.

08 · Commercial independence

Commission is not a ranking factor

Affiliate payout, EPC, expected conversion, advertiser status and the presence or absence of a commercial relationship are excluded from the intended organic ranking logic. A provider without an affiliate programme can rank first; an advertiser can rank below a non-advertiser.

Commercial placements outside the ordinary ranking should be labelled rather than blended into the research result.

09 · Robustness

We avoid pretending that one decimal point is scientific certainty

Multi-factor rankings are sensitive to the factors selected, the way data are normalised and the relative importance assigned to them. For this reason, BankRanking does not publish 9.6/10 versus 9.7/10 scores that suggest a level of measurement precision the evidence may not support.

Instead we expose the ordering, factor categories, segment assumptions and key reasons that differentiate providers. Where different reasonable assumptions could materially change the result, the correct response is to explain the sensitivity rather than hide it.

10 · Research governance

Corrections, versioning and source ownership

  • Methodology changes should be documented rather than applied silently.
  • Material factual corrections should propagate to affected ranking pages.
  • Official provider information is treated as evidence, not as editorial instruction.
  • Research-source links and affiliate destinations can be separate so a commercial redirect does not replace the evidence source.
  • Where data cannot be verified to a reasonable standard, omission or an explicit uncertainty note is preferable to invention.
