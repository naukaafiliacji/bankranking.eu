How we compare accounts

Our maintained personal-account comparisons cover Poland, Spain and Italy. Each is a selected shortlist, not a complete market survey or personalised recommendation.

What we check

We separate account maintenance from card fees, payment requirements, cash withdrawals and promotional eligibility. Official product pages are linked beside every account. The review date identifies when those sources were checked; it does not promise that terms will remain unchanged.

How accounts are ordered

The September 2026 personal-account pages use an editorial order: simple recurring-cost comparisons appear first, followed by products needing closer tariff or promotion checks. We do not claim a statistical score or universal winner. A fee filter refers only to account maintenance, not to every service or tax.

Promotions

Maximum rewards are conditional. We state material requirements and application deadlines and link to the bank for the complete rules. The homepage offer review is dated separately from the bank’s announcement.

Commercial links and corrections

Some links may earn commission. Such links are marked as sponsored in their HTML attributes; an official source link remains available for checking the evidence. Commercial availability is not evidence that a product is best.
