Product facts and historical reference lists serve different purposes.

Read the scope and record date before treating an entry as a currently available offer. The date of this policy does not mean every product or market was rechecked on that day.

1. What the current comparison covers

The Spanish personal-account comparison identifies specific account products and separates their maintenance fees, included-card costs, eligibility and promotions. It is a selection of documented products, not a complete register of all banks or account variants. Its Spanish methodology explains the user-facing scope.

Other banking categories, savings and investment pages, and the Polish and Italian lists contain reference entries alongside individually checked products. Their older records are not represented as a newly completed tariff review. An entry naming a bank or product family does not establish that a specific account is currently available.

2. Basic cost and additional services

Across the Polish, Italian and Spanish comparisons, verified maintenance amounts are stored separately from descriptions. The standard annual figure is 12 times the monthly account and card charges plus any annual card fee. A separate conditional scenario uses the stated discounted charges. Both assume 12 identical months; temporary introductory discounts are explained separately. Optional physical cards, issue or delivery charges, cash withdrawals, foreign exchange, overdrafts and other usage-dependent costs are separate.

A zero amount with conditions is distinguished from an unconditional zero. Missing or unconfirmed information is not converted to zero. If the information is insufficient for an annual figure, the comparison reports that limitation. A welcome reward is shown separately and is not deducted from ongoing maintenance.

3. How ordering and filters work

The selected sorting option explains the order applied to Spanish accounts. A cost option compares the defined maintenance figure, a promotional-benefit option compares the stated maximum amount subject to its conditions, and a provider-name option sorts alphabetically. Equal or incomplete records do not imply equal overall value.

Legacy reference lists default to provider-name order. Cost sorting uses the calculated annual account and indicated-card maintenance before recurring waivers; missing standard costs are listed last. The unconditional-free filter requires a complete zero annual maintenance figure and no fee-waiver conditions. Promotional filtering uses only quantified offers with a documented active deadline; historical prose is not parsed as a current price or bonus.

There is no calculated weighted quality model or opaque decimal score determining the displayed reference order. Historical age labels are not used to fabricate different tariff results where the record lacks verified age-specific conditions.

4. Sources and verification dates

A Spanish product record links the official product page and available tariff or promotion documentation used for its facts. Regulatory registers can identify an institution; they do not prove a particular account's fee. General comparison articles can provide research context but do not replace the product's terms.

Each detailed account shows its own verification date. Time-limited promotional amounts stop being treated as current after their recorded deadline. Changes to a product or a documented correction trigger review of the affected information. A page's editorial revision date is distinct from a product verification date.

5. Historical reference directories

The legacy Polish and Italian directories preserve research records dated 13 August 2026, except where an individual record explicitly carries another verification date. Updating this policy or shared site code does not move those record dates forward. Their sources may now show different terms.

These lists help readers identify providers and reach their documentation. They are not current-price rankings, personalized recommendations, solvency ratings or confirmation of every product's eligibility. Before applying, confirm the exact account, fees, restrictions and any promotion directly with its provider.

6. Affiliate links and sponsored placements

BankRanking may earn a commission from approved commercial links when a qualifying action occurs. This remuneration is not subtracted from a consumer's account cost, added to the consumer's bonus, or used to determine ordinary sorting. Providers can appear with direct official links before or without an affiliate agreement.

Commercial links and any separately sponsored placement are identified in context. A provider can submit documented factual corrections, but commercial payment does not determine the stated product facts. Read the Spanish affiliate explanation.

7. Corrections and practical limits

Send the affected page, disputed fact and supporting official document to contact@bankranking.eu. We review the source before changing a product record. Never send banking passwords, identity documents or full card details for an editorial correction.

Rates, fees, availability and opening requirements can change. The provider's current contractual information is the reference for an application. BankRanking supplies general information and comparisons and does not open accounts, assess a reader's financial circumstances or provide individualized financial advice.
