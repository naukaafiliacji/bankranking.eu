Cooperation

Last updated: 12 August 2026

BankRanking Europe is open to cooperation with banks, fintechs, affiliate networks, agencies and financial publishers.

Affiliate partnerships

We can integrate approved tracked links into existing bank cards while keeping the official product-source URL separate from the commercial destination.

Bank partnerships

Banks can provide product updates, official campaign information, brand assets and corrections. Commercial cooperation does not guarantee a specific editorial score.

Sponsored visibility

Future sponsored modules can be discussed, but sponsored placement should be visually distinguished from the ordinary ranking.
