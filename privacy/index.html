Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data may be processed when you use BankRanking Europe, why it may be processed, who may receive it and what rights you may have. Our approach is data-minimising: the website is currently a static comparison and information service, does not require user accounts and does not run first-party behavioural advertising or analytics in the present build.

01 · Scope & controller

Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to visitors of bankranking.eu and to people who contact BankRanking Europe directly. The data controller for personal data processed directly through the website is the operator of BankRanking Europe. Privacy enquiries can be sent to kontakt@bankranking.eu.

Operator information

If the website operator is transferred to, or formally operated by, a separate legal entity, the controller identification in this policy will be updated before any material expansion of personal-data processing.

02 · Data categories

What data may be processed

Site delivery & security data

Technical request information such as IP address, requested URL, time of request, browser/device information and security-related logs may be processed by hosting and network infrastructure.

Cookie / interface preferences

The current site stores a local preference object in your browser so that cookie-setting choices can be remembered. This remains on your device unless cleared.

Messages you send us

If you contact us, we may process your name, email address, message content and related correspondence so that we can respond and maintain appropriate business records.

Newsletter data

The current newsletter form is not connected to an email-delivery provider and does not presently transmit the entered address to a newsletter database. This policy must be updated before a live newsletter service is activated.

Affiliate / outbound click context

Where a link is later configured as a tracked affiliate link, the destination provider or affiliate network may receive referral, campaign or click information under its own privacy policy.

No user-account profiling

BankRanking currently does not create user accounts, maintain personal banking profiles or make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about website visitors.

04 · Hosting & recipients

Who may receive data

BankRanking Europe is currently delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses to GitHub Pages sites are logged and stored for security purposes. Other recipients may include domain/DNS providers, email providers used for direct correspondence, professional advisers where necessary, and banks or affiliate networks when you independently follow an outbound link.

We do not sell a visitor database to advertisers. A commercial relationship with a bank or affiliate network does not by itself give that advertiser access to correspondence you send directly to BankRanking Europe.

05 · International transfers

Processing outside the EEA

Internet infrastructure and service providers may process information in countries outside the European Economic Area. Where BankRanking Europe itself appoints a provider that processes personal data internationally, appropriate transfer mechanisms should be used where required, such as an adequacy decision or contractual safeguards.

06 · Retention

How long information is kept

We aim to keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Browser-stored preferences remain on the device until the user clears them or the preference key is replaced. Hosting/security logs are retained according to the relevant infrastructure provider's policies.

07 · Your rights

Access, correction and control

Depending on the circumstances and applicable data-protection law, you may have rights to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, consent can generally be withdrawn without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection supervisory authority in your country. To exercise rights relating to data controlled directly by BankRanking Europe, contact kontakt@bankranking.eu.

08 · Children

Children's privacy

BankRanking publishes information about youth and children's banking products, but the website is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not ask children to create accounts or submit financial profiles through the website.

09 · Security

Security and data minimisation

We use a static-site architecture and minimise direct collection of personal data. No method of internet transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we aim to limit data collection to what is proportionate to the service being provided and to use reputable infrastructure providers.

10 · Changes

Updates to this policy

This policy may change when the website introduces new functionality, analytics, a live newsletter service, additional affiliate technology or a new operating entity. Material changes should be reflected by updating the date above and, where appropriate, providing additional notice or requesting consent.
