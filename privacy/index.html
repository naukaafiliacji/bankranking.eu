The Operator controls information provided directly for contact and newsletter purposes. Questions about personal data: contact@bankranking.eu.

Information processed and why

  • Website delivery and security. Hosting and network providers may process request details such as IP address, requested URL, time and browser information to deliver and protect the service.
  • Your privacy choice. A local browser record remembers whether you allowed analytics. It remains valid for 180 days, unless you change or clear it sooner.
  • Correspondence. If you contact us, we use the contact details and message you provide to respond and keep necessary correspondence records.
  • Newsletter subscriptions. The optional newsletter form sends your email address and consent/subscription information to Brevo. Newsletter subscription is separate from agreeing to website analytics. You can unsubscribe using the link in each newsletter.
  • Optional website analytics. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 receives measurement information about visits, pages and interactions, including browser/device and referral information. It is not loaded by BankRanking before that choice. We do not enable Google Signals or advertising personalization in this site's tag configuration.

Your choices and the purposes of processing

Analytics and newsletter processing are based on your separate choices. Necessary preference storage remembers the choice you make. Delivering and securing the site, responding to enquiries and maintaining necessary business records serve the Operator's legitimate operational interests or relevant legal duties, depending on the context.

You can reject analytics and continue using the comparison. You can change or withdraw your analytics choice at any time through Cookie settings. Withdrawing it stops this site's subsequent analytics loading and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies; it does not automatically erase information already received by Google.

Recipients and external websites

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages. Newsletter submissions are processed through Brevo. Google receives analytics information only after analytics consent. Domain/DNS and correspondence providers may process information necessary for their services.

Following a bank, research or affiliate link takes you to an external website governed by its own policies. An affiliate destination may receive referral or campaign information from that link. We do not ask visitors to enter banking credentials or account application information on BankRanking.

Storage periods and international processing

The consent preference expires after 180 days. The site's Google tag is configured with a maximum cookie lifetime of 180 days without renewal on each visit; your browser may impose shorter limits. Google Analytics event-data retention is controlled separately by the Analytics property's settings. Hosting, email and newsletter records follow the relevant service configuration and applicable obligations.

We retain correspondence and subscription records for the period needed for their purposes, including maintaining unsubscribe and consent records where necessary. Infrastructure providers may process information outside the European Economic Area under their published arrangements and safeguards. Contact us for information about data controlled directly by the Operator.

Your rights and requests

Depending on the circumstances, data-protection law gives rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. You may withdraw consent without affecting processing already carried out lawfully, and complain to the competent data-protection authority. To request action concerning information controlled by BankRanking, email contact@bankranking.eu.

Scope and updates

BankRanking is an information service without visitor accounts or personal banking profiles. It does not make automated eligibility decisions about visitors. This policy will be updated when the site's processing changes. Read the Cookie and browser-storage policy for the current technical choices.
