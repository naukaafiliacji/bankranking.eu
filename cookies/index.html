Cookies and browser storage

The storage used by BankRanking, how optional analytics is controlled and how you can change your choice.

Analytics is optional and off by default. Rejecting it does not prevent you from reading comparisons or following bank links.

How analytics is controlled

The Google Analytics script is not downloaded by BankRanking until you accept the analytics option. Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization remain denied in the site's tag configuration. No BankRanking marketing-pixel category is enabled.

Your decision is remembered for 180 days. After expiry, the site asks again and keeps analytics off until you make a new choice. Preferences are specific to the browser/device; clearing browser storage resets them.

Changing or withdrawing consent

Use the privacy shield in the corner of the page, or the button on this page, at any time. When you withdraw a previously active analytics choice, the site records the refusal, disables further analytics, removes accessible Analytics cookies and refreshes the page to stop the already loaded script. Data previously sent to Google is not automatically erased by this operation.

Browser controls can also block or delete storage. LocalStorage is different from a cookie, but both involve information stored on your device, so both are described here.

Newsletter and outbound links

The newsletter is optional and uses a separate form handled by Brevo. Saving cookie preferences does not subscribe you to it. Its processing is described in our Privacy policy.

Banks and other external destinations may set their own cookies after you follow a link. The current BankRanking integration does not load a Kwanko advertising tag or claim to set a Kwanko cookie. Any future integration that changes storage or tracking will require updated information and controls.

Technical information

Google Analytics cookie documentation explains the standard cookie identifiers. The shorter 180-day period stated above is an explicit setting in BankRanking's tag, rather than Google's default period.
