Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This policy explains the browser storage used by BankRanking Europe and how we would handle analytics or marketing technologies if they are introduced later. The present build does not activate Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or BankRanking-controlled advertising cookies.

01 · Current configuration

What is stored today

The site currently uses a small browser-side preference record to remember your cookie-setting choices. It is stored through localStorage, which is technically different from a cookie but is covered by this policy because both involve storing or accessing information on a user's device.

bankranking_cookie_preferences_v1Strictly necessary preferenceRemembers the interface choices displayed in Cookie settings.Persists in the browser until cleared or replaced by a later preference version.
02 · Technologies not active

No first-party analytics or marketing tracking in this build

Analytics

GA4 and equivalent BankRanking-controlled analytics are not active in the present build.

Marketing pixels

Meta Pixel and equivalent BankRanking-controlled behavioural advertising technologies are not active.

Advertising profiles

BankRanking does not currently create behavioural advertising profiles from on-site browsing activity.

Preference panel

The interface already separates strictly necessary, analytics and marketing categories so future non-essential tools can be added only with the appropriate controls.

03 · Consent model

If non-essential tracking is introduced

Where applicable, analytics or marketing storage that is not strictly necessary should not be activated before the user has been given clear information and an appropriate opportunity to consent. Withdrawal should be as easy as giving consent, and changing Cookie settings should remain available after the initial choice.

Strictly necessary storage

EU guidance distinguishes storage that is strictly necessary for a service requested by the user from non-essential tracking. Strictly necessary technologies can be treated differently from analytics or advertising technologies, but users should still be told what they do.

04 · Third parties

External bank and affiliate websites

BankRanking contains outbound links to banks, financial institutions, regulators, research sources and, where applicable, affiliate networks. Once you leave bankranking.eu, the destination website may use its own cookies, tracking technologies or consent platform. Those technologies are controlled by the destination website, not by BankRanking Europe, and are governed by that site's own notices.

05 · Managing preferences

Your controls

You can reopen BankRanking's Cookie settings at any time. You can also clear local storage and cookies using your browser settings. Clearing browser data may reset interface preferences.

Browser controls can also block or delete cookies more broadly, but doing so can affect functionality on other websites you visit.

06 · Future changes

Adding analytics, advertising or affiliate technology

If BankRanking activates a new technology that changes the information stored on or read from your device, this policy and the cookie-preference interface should be updated before or at the time the technology is deployed. Where consent is required, the relevant technology should remain inactive until that choice is made.

07 · Contact

Questions about browser storage

Questions about this policy can be sent to kontakt@bankranking.eu. Privacy questions about personal data are covered in the Privacy Policy.
