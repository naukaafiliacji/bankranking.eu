Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Plain-language disclosure: BankRanking may earn money when a visitor clicks certain links and opens or qualifies for a bank product. This is commonly called affiliate or referral compensation.

How this affects the ranking

Commercial compensation does not automatically determine the BankRanking Score or ranking position. The scoring framework is described in the Ranking Methodology.

Where we disclose it

Ranking pages display a short disclosure near the offers, not only in the footer. Outbound commercial links use rel="sponsored" in the current build.

Sponsored placements

If BankRanking later accepts payment specifically for higher placement, that placement must be clearly labelled “Sponsored” or “Advertisement” and kept distinguishable from the editorial ranking.

Your price

Affiliate compensation does not necessarily mean a product costs more to the visitor. The provider's final pricing and terms always control.
