Ranking Methodology

Last updated: 12 August 2026

The BankRanking Score is a 0–10 editorial score intended to make products easier to compare. It is not a credit score and it is not personalized to an individual visitor.

Typical weighting framework

  • Costs — approximately 35%: account fee, card fee, waiver rules, ATM and common transaction costs.
  • Everyday usability — approximately 25%: app quality, cards, payments and account-management features.
  • Access and fit — approximately 20%: branches, cash access, travel use and the target customer profile.
  • Benefits — approximately 20%: cash bonuses, cashback, interest and other economic benefits, adjusted for difficulty, caps and duration.

Why a huge headline bonus does not automatically win

A large reward may require a high balance, payroll, a full current-account switch or multiple monthly actions. We therefore consider the conditions as well as the maximum amount.

Affiliate compensation

The ordinary editorial score should not be automatically changed by the level of affiliate commission. Any future paid placement should be labelled separately.

Verification

Each researched offer carries a verification date and an official-source link in the score details. Because bank terms change, visitors must still confirm final conditions with the provider.
