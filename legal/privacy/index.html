Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Effective date: 12 August 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how BankRanking processes personal information when you visit bankowezestawienie.pl. It is drafted for the current static version of the website. If analytics, advertising technology, user accounts, forms or additional tracking are added, this policy must be reviewed before those features are enabled.

1. Controller / operator

The data controller is the legal person or business operating BankRanking. The complete legal identity must be published in the Legal Notice / Imprint. The public contact email is kontakt@bankowezestawienie.pl.

2. Information processed

  • Technical request data: hosting and network providers may process IP address, browser/device information, requested URL, timestamps and similar server-log information needed to deliver and secure the website.
  • Contact data: if you email us, we receive the email address and information you include in the message.
  • Translation preference: the site may use session storage only to remember that you dismissed the on-page translation suggestion during the current browser session. It is not used for advertising or profiling.
  • Outbound link data: when you follow a bank or affiliate link, the destination website or affiliate-network redirect may receive ordinary web-request information and may process data under its own privacy policy.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Where EU/EEA or UK data-protection law applies, processing may rely on legitimate interests to operate, secure and improve the website; on steps taken at your request when responding to enquiries; on legal obligations where applicable; and on consent where a future feature legally requires consent.

4. Affiliate links and third parties

BankRanking may use commission-generating links. Clicking a link can take you to a bank, financial institution or affiliate network. Those third parties are independent controllers for their own websites and tracking practices. Review their privacy information before submitting an application.

5. Retention

Contact correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the enquiry, maintain business records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Hosting/security logs are retained according to the relevant hosting or infrastructure provider's operational policies. The translation-dismissal preference is session-based.

6. International transfers

Because BankRanking serves international users and may use international infrastructure or third-party destinations, personal information can be processed outside your country. Where applicable law requires safeguards for a transfer, the relevant controller or processor is responsible for applying the required transfer mechanism.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, receive portable data, withdraw consent where consent is used, and complain to a competent data-protection authority. To make a request, email kontakt@bankowezestawienie.pl.

8. No solely automated financial decision-making by BankRanking

BankRanking ranks publicly available banking products. It does not make solely automated decisions about an individual visitor that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Banks may perform their own eligibility, credit or compliance checks under their own policies.

9. Children

The service is intended for people comparing banking products and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through user accounts or forms.

10. Security

We use a static-site architecture and reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to the current service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the website, data practices, law or service providers change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.
