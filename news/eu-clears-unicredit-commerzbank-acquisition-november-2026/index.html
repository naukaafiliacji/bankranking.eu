EU clears UniCredit’s proposed takeover of Commerzbank

The Commission grants merger clearance; that does not itself complete the transaction.

Event: 16 November 2026 · Written: 23 November 2026 · BankRanking Europe

The announcement

The European Commission announced on 16 November 2026 that it had unconditionally cleared UniCredit’s proposed acquisition of Commerzbank under the EU Merger Regulation. The Commission concluded that the transaction would not raise competition concerns in the European Economic Area.

In reviewing the German market, the Commission examined overlaps between Commerzbank and UniCredit’s German business, including HypoVereinsbank. It found that customers would retain alternatives across the main retail and corporate banking services.

What it means for account customers

Merger clearance is a regulatory milestone, not proof that the proposed acquisition has already closed or that customers’ account terms have changed. Further corporate and other regulatory steps may still matter.

Account holders should follow official bank communications about any eventual operational integration and contractual changes. A merger headline by itself is not a reason to assume that a current account has been discontinued or that a deposit guarantee arrangement has immediately changed.
