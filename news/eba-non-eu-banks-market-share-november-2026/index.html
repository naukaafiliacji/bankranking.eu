Non-EU banking groups hold a growing share of EU bank assets

EBA says third-country subsidiaries accounted for 10.22% of EU bank assets in 2025.

Event: 18 November 2026 · Written: 23 November 2026 · BankRanking Europe

The announcement

On 18 November 2026, the European Banking Authority published updated analyses of subsidiaries of banking groups headquartered outside the EU and of EU banks’ foreign-currency assets and liabilities.

It reported that non-EU banking group subsidiaries accounted for 10.22% of total EU banking assets at the end of 2025, compared with 9.8% a year earlier. The EBA also noted that foreign-currency funding represented 25.3% of EU bank funding, up from 24.8% at the end of 2024.

What it means for account customers

These figures describe the structure and funding of the wider banking system. They do not directly measure the quality of an individual current account, and a foreign-owned bank is not automatically a less secure option.

When comparing banks across Europe, consider which legal entity actually holds the deposit, the deposit guarantee scheme applicable to it, and the account’s current fees. The banking group’s country of origin is not a substitute for those checks.
