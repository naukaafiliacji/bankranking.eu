EBA says EU banks are resilient but warns of cyber and credit risks

Strong capital and liquidity coexist with cyber threats and borrower vulnerabilities.

Event: 19 November 2026 · Written: 23 November 2026 · BankRanking Europe

The announcement

The European Banking Authority published its Autumn 2026 Risk Assessment Report on 19 November. It found that EU and EEA banks remained well capitalised, liquid and profitable, and that lending to the economy continued to expand.

The EBA also highlighted cyber threats, fraud, dependencies on technology suppliers, and vulnerabilities among some borrowers. It noted that banks are using more advanced AI models to improve productivity, while introducing new operational and governance challenges.

What it means for account customers

For a retail banking customer, sector-wide resilience should not be read as a promise that a particular bank will never change fees or experience an outage. Individual deposit protection and the product contract are separate from the risk assessment.

When comparing accounts, check deposit guarantee coverage, verified maintenance and card fees, how the bank responds to fraud, and whether access to cash and payments meets your needs. The report is a useful industry-wide snapshot rather than a rating of individual savings products.
