Account costs and welcome rewards

Compare monthly account and card charges first. “0 / X” shows the fee with and without the stated waiver. Expanded details show annual maintenance, eligibility, other charges and official sources. Introductory fee discounts are explained separately; transactions, taxes and optional services can cost extra.

Welcome rewards belong to the customer and may require salary credit, spending, an application code or a commitment period. Cash, vouchers and physical gifts are labelled separately. They are not deducted from annual fees. Availability may end before the advertised deadline if limits or stock are reached. This is a selected list of products, not an exhaustive market register.
